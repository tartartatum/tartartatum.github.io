WHAT LIVE TELEMETRY DOES

A Stream Deck or a keyboard only sends keys; it never knows what your ship is doing. Telemetry is the other direction: your ship tells you what it is doing, on the screens you choose.

  • Keys that show the real state. GEAR shows DOWN, MOVING or UP; DOORS show open or closed; lights, ESP, G-safe, coupled and ship power light up the way your ship really is, even when you changed them with your keyboard or in the cockpit.
  • Flight numbers off your HUD. Speed, stopping distance, radar altitude, vertical speed, G and G max, boost, fuel and quantum fuel, on a second screen, the iPad or the XENEON EDGE.
  • Warnings you cannot miss. Missile lock, taking hits, locked on and proximity flash a master caution; the ALL CLEAR pill shows the newest warning; RESET clears the flashing until the next warning.
  • Your ship and your crew. Hull, shields, power, fuel; your party's health, oxygen, ship and downed alerts on the Kneeboard (only when you share a party code).

How it works: a small free program on your PC reads the live data the game puts out (and, for drift only, the travel marker on your screen) and passes it to your own displays over your home network. It never changes game files and never presses keys by itself. Every number in the guides below is marked FROM THE GAME, CALCULATED, READ FROM YOUR SCREEN or KEPT BY THE PAGE, so you know where it comes from.

Desktop version: every deck and display also runs in a window on your PC: click the keys with your mouse, no touch screen needed. VR: full VR integration is coming soon, free.

FREE KEYS PICKER

Deck buttons with no key in your game get one automatically. The deck checks your saved keybinds and the game's default keys, then picks combinations nothing uses: up to 2 modifiers + 1 key. You check the list, change any key, or leave buttons out.

Your own keybind file is never changed. The deck backs it up, writes a separate profile, and you load that profile once in the game's Control Profiles.

Tested on one player's own keybinds: 82 deck buttons had no key, and all 82 got one (64 new free combinations, 18 the game's own default keys).

Game backup

One press copies your game's USER folder (keybinds, saved control profiles, characters) to a folder you choose, with every file checked. Restore keybinds, characters or everything; the deck asks first, refuses while the game runs, and keeps an undo copy.

Pick any folder: another drive, OneDrive or a USB stick. The newest 10 backups are kept, and the deck asks before deleting older ones. A backup only reads your game files.

How fast do you answer?

We answer every problem within 24 hours, with what is wrong and when the fix will be ready. Refund requests get an answer in the same 24 hours. Use the support page: pick your problem and send the pre-filled email.

What is the support report?

The COPY REPORT key in the CmdGlas TOC makes a short report of what is wrong: your CmdGlas programs, recent errors and a check of your PC against the system requirements. It stays on your PC; nothing is sent by itself. You read it and attach it to your support email. Your Windows user name, IP and server addresses, account and player names, e-mail addresses and keys are removed. We use it only to fix your problem and delete it once your ticket is closed.

How do I get my program?

Right after checkout, the thank-you page shows your license key and the download link. Copy the key and keep it safe. The link works for 24 hours. To download again later, open the downloads page and paste your key. Enter the key the first time the program starts.

How many PCs can I use it on?

Your key works on 2 of your own PCs. You can move it to a new PC from the program's menu.

Does the Command Deck touch game files or memory?

No. It reads your saved keybind file and sends those keys to the game window in front, the same way your keyboard does.

Buy the Command Deck here and you get both versions. The EDGE version will also be on the Elgato Marketplace. Either way, the EDGE version needs the free Command Deck EDGE Link program to send your key presses.
