Tablet quick start

Short steps for the things people do most. Want the reasons behind the choices? Read how it works.

The words in bold capitals are the buttons you press on the tablet.

Nothing found. Try one simple word, like chat, party, org or music.

Open the tablet

  1. Go to cmdglas.com in any web browser: on your PC, a laptop, an iPad or a phone.
  2. Press ENTER TABLET.
  3. On a phone, turn it sideways.

The tablet is free. You can use it on a second screen, or on the same screen as the game.

Sign in

  1. Press the ACCOUNT tab at the top.
  2. Press SIGN IN and follow the steps.

Signing in is free and gives you the full chat, party and org tools. You only sign in once on each device.

Talk in the chat

  1. Sign in first (see above).
  2. In the SOCIAL TOOLS box at the bottom right, press ENTER SOCIAL TOOLS.
  3. Open CHAT and pick #general to talk with everyone, or #party for parties.

You do not need an org for #general or #party.

Join a friend's party

  1. Ask your friend for their party code (6 letters and numbers).
  2. Go to SOCIAL TOOLS > CHAT > #party.
  3. Type the code in the CODE box and press JOIN.

To leave, press LEAVE.

Lead a party (party leaders)

  1. Go to SOCIAL TOOLS > CHAT > #party and press CREATE PARTY.
  2. Read the code out to your friends. They type it in and press JOIN.
  3. Press PARTY CMD at the top for your leader tools.
  4. To send one message to everyone: type in NETCALL and press TRANSMIT.
  5. Callsigns for everyone at once: type a name like STORM and press ASSIGN. Everyone becomes STORM 1, STORM 2 and so on.

HAND OFF gives the lead to someone else. KICK removes someone from the party.

Find people to fly with

  1. In SOCIAL TOOLS, press the GROUPS tab.
  2. Pick what you want to do, say which seats you need, and press POST IT.
  3. To join someone else: pick the seat you would fill, then press REQUEST JOIN.

Posts disappear by themselves after 12 hours.

Join an org and its chat

  1. In SOCIAL TOOLS, press the ORG tab.
  2. Under JOIN AN ORG, pick the org.
  3. Type your real RSI name and the callsign you want, then press SEND REQUEST.
  4. The org's leaders approve you. Then the org's chat opens for you in CHAT.

While you wait, #general and #party still work.

Lead an org (org leaders)

  1. Start your own org: SOCIAL TOOLS > ORG > fill in BECOME WING COMMANDER. You lead it once the org is approved.
  2. Let people in: on the ORG tab, pick their rank and squadron, then approve them.
  3. Give a squadron an order: pick the task, type the target, press SET.
  4. See everything at once: press ORG CMD at the top. NETCALL reaches everyone.

Careful: DISTRIBUTE TO ORG in the op planner sends to every member right away.

When you die or need help

  1. Press MARK KIA when you die.
  2. Press NEED RESCUE if you need a medic.
  3. Press the same button again when you are back.

An order from your leader flashes until you answer: o7 = yes, UNABLE = no (type why).

Listen to music or watch streams

  1. Press the MEDIA tab at the top.
  2. Press SPOTIFY, YOUTUBE, YT MUSIC, TWITCH or KINDLE.

Playing something in Spotify shows it under NOW PLAYING.

Ships, mining, blueprints and other tools

  1. On the home screen, look at INGAME TOOLS at the bottom left.
  2. Press a tool: BLUEPRINTS, CONTRACTS, CRAFT CALCULATOR, HARVEST, HAULING and more.
  3. Your ships: press SHIP LOADOUTS, then ADD SHIPS once.

Want something on your list? Tap rows in a tool, then press PUSH TO HUD.

Notes, routes and threat check

  1. Your own notes: the NOTES tab at the top.
  2. Routes: the ROUTE tab.
  3. Can I win that fight? The THREAT tab, or THREAT CHECK under your ship.

No address bar and the Halo over the game

The CmdGlas Tablet + Halo program shows the tablet in its own window with no browser around it, and the see-through Halo on top of the game. It never takes focus from the game. It comes free with every CmdGlas program, or on its own in the shop.

Show or hide it while you play: Shift + \ (you can change it in HOTKEYS).
